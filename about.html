Quick About Me
I’m a fourth-year Mechanical Engineering student at Northeastern University, graduating in May 2027, with a minor in Entrepreneurial Startups & Business. I’ve wanted to be an engineer for a long time, and a big part of that started with the custom LED sign business I founded in 2021. I designed the signs, engraved and assembled the products, built the website, and worked with customers. I loved the whole experience. It was so rewarding to see people enjoy the products I created, and it is the main reason why I decided to go into engineering.
At Northeastern, I’ve been able to keep building on that through classes, lab work, design projects, and my co-op. I completed my first co-op with the Town of Wellesley Engineering Division as a Mechanical/Construction Engineering Co-op, and I’m still working there part-time during the school year. My work has included AutoCAD design work, creating plan sets, taking field measurements, writing Python automation tools, digitizing office workflows, and designing ArcGIS inspection workflows. I’ve really enjoyed both collecting data and then turning it into drawings, systems, and tools that the other engineers can use.
Outside of engineering, I’m happiest when I’m doing something active. I’m currently training for the Boston Half Marathon, since I love to run, along with skiing and swimming. I like music, hiking, cars, and spending time outside when the Boston weather is finally nice out. I studied abroad in Madrid during my first semester of college, which helped me become more comfortable speaking Spanish and gave me a chance to travel, meet new people, and see more of the world early in college. As I finish my senior year and work on my capstone project, I’m excited to keep learning, contribute to a great engineering team, and keep doing work I’m proud of.
