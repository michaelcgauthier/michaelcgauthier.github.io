About me

Hi, I’m Michael! I’m a fourth-year Mechanical Engineering student at Northeastern University with a minor in Entrepreneurial Startups & Business. I’m currently balancing classes, my capstone project, and continued work as a mechanical/construction engineering co-op, where I’ve been creating AutoCAD plan sets, survey-based design drawings, Python automation tools, and ArcGIS inspection workflows. I genuinely love what I do at my at Northeastern, and in my internship, I’m excited to keep growing as an engineer as I start my career!
