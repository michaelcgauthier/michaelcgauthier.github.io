About me

I’m a Mechanical Engineering major at Northeastern University. I genuinely love what I do and I’m happiest when I’m building something that has a clear purpose and a clean end result. Right now I’m working as a Mechanical and Construction Engineering co op. I started full time last fall, and they asked me to stay on, so I’m balancing engineering work and classes.

My day to day work is a mix of design and execution: AutoCAD plan sets, georeferenced base layers, field measurement, and workflow automation. I’ve built Python tools that batch process engineering files faster, and ArcGIS inspection workflows that standardize field data collection and make it usable immediately for engineering review. I like work that connects constraints, data, and design decisions in a practical way.
